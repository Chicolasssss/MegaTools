## What is an Image Compressor?

An image compressor reduces the file size of your photos and graphics without significantly degrading their visual quality. It achieves this by stripping out unnecessary metadata and intelligently grouping similar colored pixels.

### The Importance of Image Compression for SEO

Large images are the number one cause of slow-loading websites. Google's Core Web Vitals algorithm heavily penalizes slow pages. By compressing your JPG and PNG files before uploading them, you ensure lightning-fast load times, improving both user experience and your SEO rankings.
