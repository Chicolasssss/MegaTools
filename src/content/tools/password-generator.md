## What is a Random Password Generator?

A Random Password Generator is a critical security tool that creates complex, unpredictable combinations of characters, numbers, and symbols. In today's digital landscape, using weak or reused passwords is the primary cause of account breaches.

### Why You Need a Strong Password

Cybercriminals use automated tools to guess millions of passwords per second. A password like 'admin123' can be cracked instantly, while a 16-character string combining mixed case, numbers, and symbols would take trillions of years to decipher. This tool guarantees cryptographically secure generation done entirely on your browser—no data is sent to our servers.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Is this password generator safe to use?
Yes. The generation happens entirely within your browser using JavaScript's secure randomness APIs. Your passwords are never transmitted over the internet or saved anywhere.

#### How long should my password be?
Security experts recommend a minimum of 16 characters for critical accounts like email or banking, and at least 12 characters for general use.
