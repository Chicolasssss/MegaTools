## What is a Word Counter?

A Word Counter is a crucial writing assistant that tracks the length and structure of your documents. It provides real-time metrics on words, characters, and paragraphs, making it indispensable for meeting strict word count requirements.

### Why Character Counting Matters for SEO and Social Media

Platforms like Twitter have strict character limits, while Google search results truncate meta descriptions over 160 characters. Furthermore, SEO specialists rely on word counts to ensure articles have enough depth (typically 1,000+ words) to rank competitively on search engine result pages (SERPs).

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How is reading time calculated?
Reading time is estimated based on the average adult reading speed of 250 words per minute.

#### Does the character count include spaces?
Our tool provides both metrics: total characters including spaces, and a separate count excluding spaces.
