## What is a QR Code Generator?

A QR Code (Quick Response Code) Generator is a utility that transforms URLs, text, or contact information into a scannable 2D barcode. Modern smartphones can read these codes natively through their camera apps.

### Bridging Offline and Online Marketing

QR codes are the perfect bridge between physical print marketing (flyers, menus, business cards) and your digital presence. By generating a QR code that links to a UTM-tagged URL, you can perfectly track the ROI of your offline marketing campaigns in Google Analytics.

#### Do these QR codes expire?
No. The QR codes generated here are 'Static' QR codes. The data is encoded directly into the image itself, meaning they will work forever as long as the destination URL remains active.
