## What is a UTM Builder?

A UTM (Urchin Tracking Module) Builder is an essential marketing utility that attaches special tracking tags to your URLs. These tags allow analytics platforms like Google Analytics to accurately identify where your traffic is coming from.

### Best Practices for UTM Parameters

When running digital marketing campaigns across social media, email, and CPC networks, consistency is key. Always use lowercase letters, avoid spaces (use underscores or hyphens), and keep a standardized naming convention so your marketing reports remain clean and actionable.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Do UTM parameters affect SEO?
No. Search engines ignore UTM parameters when indexing pages. However, it's recommended to use canonical tags on your website to ensure search engines know the original URL.

#### What are the required parameters?
Generally, only 'utm_source' is strictly required, but it's highly recommended to use 'utm_medium' and 'utm_campaign' as well for proper attribution.
