## What is a URL Slug?

A URL slug is the exact address of a specific webpage on your site. For example, in `https://example.com/blog/my-first-post`, the slug is `my-first-post`. Using clean, readable, and keyword-rich slugs is a crucial part of On-Page SEO.

## How to use this tool

Type or paste your article title into the input box. The tool will automatically:
1. Convert all characters to lowercase.
2. Remove accents and diacritics (e.g., "café" becomes "cafe").
3. Strip out special characters, punctuation, and emojis.
4. Replace all spaces with hyphens (`-`).

The resulting slug is ready to be used in your CMS, WordPress, or custom web application.

### Why do slugs matter for SEO?
Search engines like Google read the words in your URL to understand what the page is about. A clean slug like `url-slug-generator` performs much better than a messy URL with parameters like `?p=123&title=url%20slug`.
