## Stay Secure Online

In today's digital landscape, security is paramount. Our security tools are designed with a strict privacy-first approach. 

### Why Use Client-Side Security Tools?
Every tool in this category runs entirely within your web browser. When you generate a password or calculate a SHA-256 hash, the raw data never leaves your device. This guarantees that your sensitive information cannot be intercepted or logged by our servers.
